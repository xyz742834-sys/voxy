## 2. Every-frame submission, composite and lifetime

**Own-fence / same-queue / target-barrier sufficiency — CONFIRMED for the inspected ordinary-frame protocol.** `drawEveryFrame` waits successfully through `VkFrameTracker.waitForFrame()` before request servicing, the scene UBO write and `prepare` (`McNativeHierarchicalLoad.java:426–435`). `prepare` writes the scene-owned node/geometry/traversal buffers and resets requests (`VkHierarchicalScene.java:714–724`); these are used by Voxy's submission, not Minecraft's composite. The buffers are HOST_VISIBLE/HOST_COHERENT (`VkBuffer.java:72–81`). The subsequent queue submission supplies host-to-device ordering. A second wait at `beginFrame` precedes command-buffer/scratch reuse (`VkFrameTracker.java:115–158`; `VkUploadStream.java:163`). New scene construction writes newly allocated scene resources; it does not overwrite the retired scene's resources.

Minecraft's composite references its pipeline/descriptors/sampler and Voxy's target colour/depth; its descriptors are fixed, and it does not read the mutable scene UBO, tables, geometry or model buffers (`McNativeComposite.java:45–63`, `:89–95`; `native_composite.frag:18–23`). Waiting for Voxy's fence alone would not finish a later Minecraft composite. The other necessary edge is present: Voxy adopts `mc.graphicsQueue()` (`McNativeVkContext.java:114`); Minecraft submits its frame at the end of `runTick` before the next frame (`.agent-run/r25/mc-client.javap:3273–3275`). Target reuse transitions outside rendering have ALL_COMMANDS source stages and attachment read/write destination scopes (`VkRenderTarget.java:107–114`, `VkTexture.java:187–207`). They order the previous submitted composite's fragment reads before target reuse. `prepareSources` then publishes Voxy's target writes to future fragment sampling and transitions both images to SHADER_READ_ONLY_OPTIMAL (`McNativeComposite.java:71–77`). Minecraft's current composite is submitted after that Voxy submission. Queue order supplies the scope; the barriers supply the dependencies.

This reasoning follows the [Vulkan pipeline-barrier contract](https://docs.vulkan.org/refpages/latest/refpages/source/vkCmdPipelineBarrier.html) and [host-write/submission rules](https://docs.vulkan.org/spec/latest/chapters/synchronization.html#synchronization-submission-host-writes). It is a source/specification judgment plus retained-run consistency, not new hardware overlap testing.

**Request servicing — CONFIRMED for a reused scene on successful paths.** `requestsUnread` is set after ordinary-frame submission (`:439`) and consumed after a successful wait before the next prepare (`:427–429`). A handed sample consumes any outstanding ordinary requests after checked device-idle (`:323–331`) and services each of its three submissions after its fence (`:343–346`). `python3 .agent-run/r25/frame_contract.py` executes the unchanged ordinary body and handed loop against a submission oracle: eight submissions, seven serviced exactly once, final ordinary submission still unread; no host write before its wait, no prepare before prior requests are serviced. Retiring a scene discards its outstanding requests; this is not a claim that every retired submission is serviced.

**Retirement and shutdown — CONFIRMED for submitted work and the reproduced context-release sequence.** Ordinary retirement keeps both the scene and composite target alive on Minecraft's destruction queue (`:647–667`). The actual 26.2 dependency's encoder submits, waits for submit index minus two, and only then rotates that queue (`mc-encoder-dependency.javap:436–499`); a queued scene's earlier Voxy submission is also before that completion signal on the same queue. Encoder destruction closes the remaining submission builder, waits for graphics-queue idle and drains the queue (`:163–183`). Thus ordinary destruction covers both Voxy and Minecraft submitted references. Immediate release checks device-idle success and matching owner device before destroying current and already-queued owners; it then stops the tracker before global caches/context are cleared (`McNativeVkContext.java:293–337`; hierarchy `:670–699`). Failed idle checks avoid destruction; device divergence/queue failure deliberately leak and report it (`:210–215`, `:653–656`, `:677–682`). No unchecked device-idle result is substituted for the every-frame fence.

A device-idle wait does not finish an unsubmitted Minecraft batch. Normal frame-end submission is source-confirmed; arbitrary interruption between composite recording and Minecraft submission, stopping-event ordering under such interruption, and fault-injected shutdown remain unverified. No unconditional all-exception shutdown guarantee is awarded.
